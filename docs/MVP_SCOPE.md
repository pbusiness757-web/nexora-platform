# MVP SCOPE

## PURPOSE

Define exactly what is included in Version 1.

The goal is to launch quickly, get first real clients and process real transactions.

Everything outside this document is postponed.

---

# MVP OBJECTIVE

Launch a working Crypto-to-Bank payout platform.

Primary focus:

* Business payouts
* Corporate bank account payouts
* Supplier payments
* Contractor payments
* Individual payouts

Supported countries:

* Russia
* Kazakhstan
* Uzbekistan
* Azerbaijan
* Kyrgyzstan

# SUCCESS CRITERIA

The MVP is successful when:

* Real clients create requests
* Real crypto is received
* Real payouts are completed
* Business clients contact the platform
* Operators can manage requests efficiently

---

# MVP RULE

Every feature must answer:

Will this help us process real payouts faster?

If not, it does not belong in MVP.
