# DESIGN SYSTEM

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

PayBridge is not a crypto exchange.

PayBridge is a financial infrastructure platform.

The website must feel like a modern international fintech company.

Users should immediately trust the platform for business payments and corporate settlements.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

1. Trust First
2. Business Focused
3. Premium Experience
4. Modern Financial Technology
5. Fast and Professional

---

# VISUAL STYLE

Style:

Premium Fintech

Mix of:

* Stripe
* Wise
* Revolut
* Modern Banking

Avoid:

* Crypto exchange style
* Casino colors
* Flashy gradients
* Trading charts everywhere
* Bitcoin coins floating on screen
