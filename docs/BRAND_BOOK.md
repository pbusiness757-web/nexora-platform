# BRAND POSITIONING

CryptoPayout is a premium Crypto-to-Bank payout infrastructure platform.

The platform enables individuals and businesses to convert cryptocurrency into local fiat currency and receive payouts to:

* Bank Cards
* Personal Bank Accounts
* Corporate Bank Accounts
* Electronic Wallets

Special focus:

Business and Corporate Payouts.
